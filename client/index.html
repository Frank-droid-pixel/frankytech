Is FRANKY TECH free to start?
Yes. You can create an account and start on the Free plan with no card required. You can upgrade at any time as your business grows.
Is my business data kept separate from other businesses?
Yes. Every record you create is tied to your business account, and every request is checked for authentication, authorization and ownership before any data is returned.
Can I use FRANKY TECH on my phone?
Yes. FRANKY TECH is fully responsive and designed to work well on phones, tablets, laptops and desktops.
